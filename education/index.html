Education creators ask

What counts as an education creator here?

Someone who teaches their audience how to use technology: new AI models, apps and software. Tutorials, walkthroughs, comparisons and workflows all count.

What is a sponsored integration?

A segment about a sponsor inside your normal video. It is different from a dedicated tutorial, which is wholly about the product, and it should be priced differently.

Can I refuse to say a sponsor's script?

You can agree on required points and keep your own wording. Claims should be true and match your honest experience with the product.

Can I show a tool's weak points in a sponsored tutorial?

Settle it before you sign. We ask for the right to be honest about limits, and for the brand to check facts rather than opinions.
