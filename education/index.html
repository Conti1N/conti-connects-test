Education creators ask

What is a sponsored integration?

A segment about a sponsor inside your normal video. It is different from a dedicated video, which is wholly about the product, and it should be priced differently.

Can I refuse to say a sponsor's script?

You can agree on required points and keep your own wording. Claims should be true and match your honest experience with the product.

Do you take student creators?

Students can apply. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian involved from the first conversation.
