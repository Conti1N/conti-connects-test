Influencer management FAQ

Plain answers for creators thinking about a manager and brands thinking about a creator.

  1. Home
  2. FAQ
What is influencer talent management?

Influencer talent management is the business side of a creator's career, run by someone else: finding brand deals, negotiating rates and contracts, managing deadlines and making sure the creator is paid.

How many followers do I need to get a manager?

There is no set number. Managers look at niche, engagement, posting habits and whether brands already want to work with you. Read the guide.

What is the difference between a talent agency and an influencer marketing agency?

A talent agency or manager works for the creator. An influencer marketing agency is hired by the brand to run a campaign. Compare them.

What does exclusivity mean in a brand deal?

It means you agree not to work with named competitors for a set time. It limits your income, so the competitors, the dates and the fee should all be written down.

What are usage rights?

Usage rights say where a brand can use your content beyond your own post, such as its website, emails or paid ads, and for how long.

Do gifted products have to be disclosed?

In the US, yes. A free product can be a material connection, and the FTC expects it to be disclosed clearly when you post about it.

Do you work with creators outside fashion, travel and education?

Not at the moment.

Do you work with brands directly?

Yes. Brands can send a brief to work with fashion, travel and education creators.
