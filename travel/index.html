Travel creators ask

What is a press trip?

A trip where a hotel, operator or tourism board hosts a creator in exchange for agreed content. Some pay a fee as well. Costs, deliverables and dates should be in writing before you book travel.

Do I have to disclose a free stay?

Yes. In the US, a free or discounted stay is a material connection, so it must be disclosed clearly. See our sponsored content policy.

How early should I pitch a trip?

Start as soon as your dates are firm. Hotels and tourism boards plan around seasons, and late requests often miss the budget.
