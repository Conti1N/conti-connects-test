Brands ask

What types of creators can we work with?

Fashion, travel and education creators on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

What should we send first?

The product, the goal, the country, your dates and any must-haves. A rough budget range helps us suggest creators who fit it.

Who owns the content?

That depends on the agreement. Posting, reposting and paid ads are different permissions, so say early if you want to run the content as ads.

Will posts be labeled as ads?

Yes. Creators must clearly disclose paid posts, gifts and free stays. Read the sponsored content policy.
