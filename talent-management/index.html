Before you sign with anyone

What does a talent manager handle?

Finding brand deals, pitching, rate and contract negotiation, usage rights, scheduling, briefs and payment follow-up. Filming, voice and creative choices stay with the creator.

Is a talent manager the same as an agent?

Not exactly. An agent mainly finds and books work. A manager also guides the wider business. In some US states, people who procure work for talent need a license, so ask anyone you sign with what they do and where.

Should I pay a manager up front?

Be careful with anyone who asks for fees before doing any work. Managers are commonly paid from deals they help close. Get the terms in writing and read them before you sign.
