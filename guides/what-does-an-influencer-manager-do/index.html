What does an influencer manager do?

An influencer manager runs the business side of a creator's brand deals: pitching, negotiating, scheduling and getting paid.

By Ismail Nur, founder. Updated November 24, 2026.

Apply for management
  1. Home

  3. What does an influencer manager do?

The main jobs

  • Pitching. Contacting brands that fit and making the case for the creator.
  • Pricing. Setting a rate for each piece of work, and for extras such as ad usage.
  • Negotiating. Settling fee, deliverables, revisions, exclusivity and dates.
  • Running the deal. Keeping the brief, drafts and approvals moving.
  • Payment. Sending or chasing invoices.

What a manager should not take over

Your creative work, your account passwords and your right to say no. You should see every real offer, including the ones your manager would turn down.

When you need one

A manager starts to make sense when brand messages take time away from making content, when you keep accepting the first number, or when contracts arrive that you don't understand. If you get one offer a year, a template and a careful read may be enough.

Questions to ask before signing

  1. How are you paid, and on which deals?
  2. Is it exclusive? How long does it last, and how do I leave?
  3. Who holds the money, and when do I get it?
  4. Which brands will you contact first, and why?
  5. Do you need a license for this work where I live?

Have a lawyer read any contract before you sign it.
