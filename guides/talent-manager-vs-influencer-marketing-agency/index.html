Talent manager vs influencer marketing agency

A talent manager works for the creator. An influencer marketing agency works for the brand.

By Ismail Nur, founder. Updated November 24, 2026.

Which one do you need?

If you are a creator who wants someone on your side, look for a manager or agent. If you are a brand that needs a whole campaign planned across many creators, look for an influencer marketing agency. If you are a brand that wants to work with particular creators, go to whoever manages them.

Can one company do both?

Some do. Ask who they act for in your deal, and whether they are paid by both sides. You should know that before you agree to a price.
