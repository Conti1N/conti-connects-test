How many followers do you need to get a manager?

There is no set number. A small account with a clear niche and real replies can be easier to place than a large, unfocused one.

By Ismail Nur, co-founder. Updated November 24, 2026.

What managers look at instead

  • Niche. Can someone say what you make in one sentence?
  • Response. Comments, saves and shares from real people.
  • Audience. Where viewers live, because brands buy by country.
  • Reliability. A steady posting record.
  • Proof. A past brand post, or a strong unpaid post about a product.

Some agencies do set minimums

Rules vary. Some firms publish a subscriber floor, some take applications from anyone, and some work by referral only. Check each agency's own application page, because the numbers change.

What to do before you apply

  1. Pin three posts that show your best work.
  2. Put a business email in your bio.
  3. Write one line on who watches you.
  4. Screenshot your audience countries and ages.
  5. List ten brands you already use.

Then apply to CCM Talent if you make fashion, travel or education content.
