About Conti Connects

Questions, answered

What does Conti Connects do?

Conti Connects is a talent management agency for fashion, travel and education creators. We pitch creators to brands, negotiate the terms, and keep the brief, deadlines and payment on track. Brands can also come to us to work with creators.

Who is Conti Connects for?

Creators on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube who make fashion, travel or education content and want help with brand deals, and brands in those fields that want to work with them.

What does an influencer talent manager do?

A talent manager finds and handles business for a creator: pitching brands, setting rates, negotiating contracts and usage rights, and making sure the work and the payment both arrive. Read the full guide.

How can a brand work with a creator through Conti Connects?

Send the product, the niche and the timing. We suggest creators who fit, share their availability and terms, and manage the details through to posting.
