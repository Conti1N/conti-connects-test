What we handle

  • Gifting or paidWe separate product gifting from paid work and agree what each one includes.
  • Usage rightsFashion brands often want your photos for ads. That is a separate term, with its own price and end date.
  • ExclusivityIf a brand wants you off its rivals, we pin down which ones and for how long.
  • Try-on timelinesSizes, shipping and returns are settled before your posting date.

Brands that fit fashion creators

We look for brands whose clothes you would wear without being paid:

  • Streetwear and menswear labels
  • Footwear and accessories
  • Thrift, resale and vintage
  • Grooming and fragrance

Are you one of these brands? See how to work with fashion creators.

Fashion creators ask

Should I accept gifted clothes?

It can make sense if you want the piece and nothing is required in return. If the brand expects a post, agree what it gets. You must still disclose gifted products when you post about them.

Can a brand use my outfit photo in its ads?

Only if you agree to it. Ad use, sometimes called whitelisting or paid usage, should be written into the deal with a time limit.

Which fashion creators can apply?

Streetwear, menswear, womenswear, thrift and styling creators on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
